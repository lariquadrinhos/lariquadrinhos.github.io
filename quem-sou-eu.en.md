I'm a UX Designer, and I like working where people, logic and creativity meet. My interest goes beyond the interface: I want to understand the problem, the context and everything that shapes an experience, so I can make design decisions that truly make sense. I came from Biochemical Engineering and brought with me a scientist's curiosity, logical reasoning and an eye for processes and systems.

Not knowing something is usually the beginning of a discovery. When I come across something I don't yet understand, my reaction is to want to get closer. Maybe that's why learning, for me, has less to do with piling up knowledge and more to do with widening what I'm able to understand.

There's something very special, to me, about the moment a question changes the way we see a problem and opens a path that wasn't there before. That's the kind of shift I want my work to spark.

## My values

### Love

Love, to me, is a way of being in the world. It's the care I put into the things I do, the people I relate to and what I choose to build.

### Curiosity

I want to understand. I like asking questions, finding out how things work and getting into subjects I don't know yet. Unknown worlds don't scare me; they draw me in.

### Movement

The world is changing all the time, and so am I. I like to set things in motion: a question can change an idea, a conversation can open a path, and a small change can transform a whole process.

### Best possible

I believe we can leave the world a better place than we found it. *Best possible* is a motto that means: do the best I can now, learn from it, and keep looking for a better possible tomorrow.

### Positive impact

I like the idea that our actions can leave small good marks wherever we go. Before acting, I think about the best way to add something positive.

### Questioning

To me, questioning isn't about tearing down what already exists; it's about making room to discover what could still exist.
